Prepared 30 October 2026

Staging:  https://virtualofficeangels.com.au/staging/1384/
Time:     about 20 minutes


WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
Staging's page sitemap (the list Google reads) still lists 24 pages from the old website, such as
About Us, Contact Us, FAQ and Home-old. Each one already redirects to its new page, so visitors never
see them, but Google would keep being told they exist. They also make duplicates: About Us and About,
Contact Us and Contact, FAQ and FAQs.

  - The 24 old pages go to the Trash. Nothing is lost: every one has a redirect (checked against
    redirects.csv on 30 October), and the Trash keeps them for 30 days.
  - The Thank You page (shown after the contact form) is hidden from search, so it leaves the sitemap.

The menus are built by the theme, not from these pages, so trashing them does not change any menu.

STEP 2: HIDE THE THANK YOU PAGE FROM SEARCH
-------------------------------------------
1. Pages > Thank You > Edit.
2. In the Yoast SEO box (below the content, or the Yoast icon on the right): Advanced.
3. "Allow search engines to show this Page in search results?" > No.
4. Update.


STEP 3: CHECK REDIRECTION DID NOT ADD ANYTHING
----------------------------------------------
Redirection can add a redirect by itself when a page's address changes, and trashing a page changes
its address (WordPress adds __trashed to the end).

1. Tools > Redirection > Redirects. Type __trashed in the search box > Search.
2. If any rows appear: tick them > Bulk actions > Delete > Apply. Tell me how many there were.
3. Clear the search. The total should be the same as before (155).


STEP 4: CLEAR THE CACHE
-----------------------
Admin bar > Caching > Purge All.


STEP 5: TELL ME
---------------
Tell me when you're done. I then check from here:
  - the page sitemap lists 13 pages: the home page, About, Services, How It Works, Managed Virtual
    Support, Client Stories, Videos, FAQs, Contact, Client Case Studies and the three city pages
    (Insights is listed in the post sitemap, as the articles page)
  - the Thank You page is out of the sitemap and tells search engines not to list it
  - the 24 old addresses still redirect to their new pages


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- A page you need went to the Trash: Pages > Trash > hover over it > Restore.
- Something else breaks: restore the backup from the start (UpdraftPlus > Existing backups >
  Restore > Database).


ON LAUNCH DAY
-------------
Nothing extra: launch copies staging to live, so live gets the cleaned-up pages and sitemap too.
Live's own old pages are replaced in the same step.
