## Why these cities

These are the three cities where Virtual Office Angels has active clients today, one in each state
that has any.

- **Cities, not states.** The owner asked for areas smaller than a state; New South Wales is too
  large. People also tend to search by city ("virtual assistant Sydney") rather than by state.
- **Not suburbs.** Very few people search for a virtual assistant by suburb, and each suburb would
  have only one or two clients.
- **Sydney** has 7 of the 11 active clients and 10 of the 14 VAs, plus the office in Chatswood.
- **Brisbane** shares its page with the Gold Coast: the two are about an hour apart, and both clients
  are in finance.
- **Melbourne** has one client, so its page is the thinnest. It should grow as clients are added
  there.
- **Not included:** Wollongong (one client, Hokus E-bikes) and every city with no active client.

The ranking may change once Google Keyword Planner shows how many people search each city.

## Rules and things to know

The biggest risk is three pages that read the same with only the city name swapped. Google calls
these "doorway pages" and can push the whole site down for them.

- **Make each page genuinely local.** Write about that city's clients, industries, hours and story.
  If a sentence would work on all three pages, it belongs on the main service pages instead.
- **Only real facts.** No invented figures, client results, testimonials, awards or people. If
  something is missing, leave a [placeholder] and add it to Open questions.
- **Client permission first.** Name a client, show a logo or tell a client's story only once the
  client has agreed. Until then, describe them, for example "a financial planning practice in
  Toowong".
- **Say "Virtual Office Angels" in full,** never "VOA".
- **Australian English** (organise, specialised).
- **Keep it short.** The site uses short, high-impact copy: brief headings and paragraphs of two or
  three sentences.
- **Link to the service pages** rather than repeating them: each service already has its own page.
- **Search phrases are not confirmed yet.** "Virtual assistant Sydney" (and Brisbane, Melbourne,
  Gold Coast) are the likely ones. Keyword Planner numbers will confirm them later, so use them
  naturally and don't stuff them in.
